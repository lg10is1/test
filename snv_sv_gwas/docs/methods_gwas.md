# Methods template: SV/SNV GWAS analysis

## English

Genome-wide association summary statistics were generated using GCTA fastGWA mixed linear model analysis for a binary phenotype (`fastGWA-mlm-binary`). Genotypes were encoded as hard-called additive allele dosages. The model included a sparse genetic relationship matrix to account for relatedness and adjusted for 20 genetic principal components supplied as quantitative covariates. Additional fixed covariates, such as sequencing or calling batch, were included when available. Variant-level filters used in the GCTA command included `--geno 0.1` and `--maf 0.01`.

GWAS summary statistics were then analyzed separately for small variants and structural variants. PanGenie was treated as a single call set, represented by the `set00` example in this public workflow. Variants were restricted to autosomes, variants within 1 Mb of chromosome ends were removed, and variants were classified as structural variants when the maximum allele length was at least 50 bp; remaining variants were classified as SNVs/indels. DeepVariant SNV/indel and Paragraph SV GWAS summary statistics were standardized to the same chromosome, position, variant ID, and P-value format. Independent association signals were identified using PLINK clumping with a primary significance threshold of P < 5 × 10^-6, an LD threshold of r² < 0.01, and a 1 Mb window. For PanGenie, the secondary clumping threshold was P < 0.05; for DeepVariant and Paragraph, the secondary threshold was P < 1. Manhattan and QQ plots were generated from standardized autosomal GWAS results, and genomic inflation factors were calculated from the median chi-square statistic.
